Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

The apps

This policy covers rAuthenticator for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Linux. The apps do not collect any data.

On Linux the accounts are stored in your desktop keyring through the Secret Service (GNOME Keyring, KWallet or KeePassXC). The app lock uses polkit (your login password or fingerprint) in the .deb, or a passphrase whose salted hash is kept in that keyring in the AppImage. Screen scans go through your desktop's screenshot portal, and the screenshot is deleted as soon as it has been read. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • No account, no server. There is no sign-in, and the apps make no network connections: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no update checks, no cloud backup. They contain no third-party code.
  • Your accounts stay on your device. The two-factor secrets you add are stored in your device's Keychain and never leave it, except when you yourself show them as a QR code or setup key to move them to another app or device.
  • Camera and screen. The camera (and on the Mac, Screen Recording) is used only while you scan a QR code. The images are read on the device and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Face ID and Touch ID are handled by Apple's system; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.
  • Clipboard. A copied code goes to the clipboard of this device only (not to other devices) and is cleared after 60 seconds.
  • Settings such as Hide codes and the app lock are stored on the device.
  • Children. The apps are not directed at children and collect nothing from anyone.

This website

  • This website tracks nothing. There are no analytics, no cookies, no trackers, no ads and no third-party scripts; the pages load nothing from other sites.
  • The site is served by Cloudflare, Inc. and copied to GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); like any web host, they may process your IP address and request details in their own server logs to deliver and protect the site. We do not use those logs to count or profile visitors.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the apps do. Questions go to the support page.
