Support

Help for rAuthenticator on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Get in touch

Report a problem or ask a question on GitHub Issues (a free GitHub account is needed to post). Please say which device, which system version and which app version you use (on iPhone and iPad: Settings in the app shows it). Never post a QR code, setup key or code: anyone who sees one can generate your codes.

Moving accounts in

  • From Google Authenticator: on the phone, open Google Authenticator, then ☰ → Transfer accounts → Export accounts, and pick all accounts or a few. Scan the codes it shows with rAuthenticator (+ → Scan). The phone keeps its accounts.
  • From a service's own QR code: scan it with the camera, from a photo or screenshot, or on the Mac from the screen.
  • By setup key: + → Enter Setup Key, and type the key the service shows next to its QR code.
  • Between your devices: Export All on one shows transfer QR codes; scan them on the other.

Questions

  • Which codes work? Time-based (TOTP) and counter-based (HOTP), SHA-1, SHA-256 and SHA-512, 6 to 8 digits, any period. The transfer format cannot carry 7-digit codes or periods other than 30 seconds; those export one by one instead.
  • Why does it ask for the camera or Screen Recording? Only the scanners use them, only while you scan, and nothing leaves the device.
  • I scanned the same codes twice. Accounts you already have are skipped, even after you renamed them.
  • How do I remove everything? Delete the app. On the Mac, also delete its two items in Keychain Access (search for "authenticator").
  • Is my data sent anywhere? No. See the privacy policy.
